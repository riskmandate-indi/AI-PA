## Recommendation: Darshi

- Meaning: "one who sees". It fits the origin story. A PA that saw both the ticket (18th) and the calendar (19th) would have caught the error.
- Sound: two syllables, stress on the first, open vowels, soft "sh". Calm, not alarming.
- Spelling: written as it sounds. Six letters.
- Values: sees for you, on your phone. Foresight and care without a surveillance feel.
- Clearance: the only strong candidate with no same-name app or brand in my search.
- Tagline idea: "Darshi sees it first."

Before this is final:
- Check domains (darshi.app, darshi.ai, getdarshi.com). All unchecked.
- Run a proper trademark search (UKIPO, EUIPO, USPTO, India). Not done here.
- Search the App Store directly for "Darshi".
- Ask a native Sanskrit or Hindi speaker to confirm tone.

Runners-up: Nitya, then Memor.
